## 1. What each dataset actually is (and is not)

| Dataset | What it is | Coverage / resolution | What it is NOT | Honest label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PREM** (Dziewonski & Anderson 1981) | 1-D reference: ρ, Vp, Vs, Q_μ, Q_κ as piecewise **polynomials in radius**. First-order jumps at 15 / 24.4 / 220 / 400 / 670 / 2891 / 5149.5 km depth (*measured* in `prem.nd`), plus polynomial-only boundaries at 80 and 600 km | Whole Earth, radial only. Transversely isotropic 24.4–220 km | Lateral structure. Hot/cold mantle. Slabs. Any 3-D tomography | *reference model* |
| **CRUST1.0** (Laske, Masters, Ma & Pasyanos 2013) | Per 1°×1° cell: tops of 9 layers (water, ice, 3 sediment, 3 crystalline crust, then Moho) with Vp, Vs, ρ in each | Global, 64 800 cells. Where there is no local data, cells are filled by **crustal-type extrapolation** | A measurement at every cell. Anything below the Moho except one uppermost-mantle value | *compilation* |
| **Slab2** (Hayes et al. 2018, USGS) | Depth, dip, strike, thickness and **uncertainty** grids of the subducting slab surface | 27 subduction zones, ~0.05° grids | Global mantle. Slab below where seismicity and tomography constrain it (the `unc` grid says where) | *observed geometry + uncertainty* |
| **SCEC CFM 7.0** (Plesch, Marshall & Shaw 2024) | 608 triangulated 3-D fault surfaces (556 preferred, 39 alternative, 13 rupture) + per-fault metadata | **California only.** Southern CA is mature; central/northern CA additions are *preliminary pending community evaluation* (the release says so) | A global fault model. A slip-rate or hazard model | *regional community model* |
| **CHAOS-8** (Finlay, Kloss, Olsen et al., DTU) | Spherical-harmonic geomagnetic field from Swarm/CHAMP/Ørsted + observatories: **time-dependent core field n ≤ 20** (B-spline in time) + **static lithospheric field n = 21–185** | 1997 → present (the core part past the last data is extrapolated) | A dynamo. The external/ionospheric field (separate model parts) | *observation-derived* |

**The geographic mismatch is the first design fact.** PREM, CRUST1.0 and
CHAOS are global. Slab2 is the world's 27 trenches. CFM is one state. The
interior view is therefore **global by default with two regional zoom-ins**,
not one uniform 3-D Earth. Pretending CFM-style fault detail exists
everywhere would be the Moon-terrain trap (synthesised detail indistinguishable
from data).


Shape rules, matching the rest of the repo: kernels are pure and node-gated,
the renderer computes nothing, assets are baked (no runtime proxy for
static models), lazy-loaded by layer, and a dead asset draws nothing and says
so.

**`column.js` is the product.** "Pick any point and see the Earth beneath
it": ocean → CRUST1.0 layers → Moho → (Slab2 surface, if inside one of the 27
zones, at its depth ± `unc`) → PREM mantle → CMB (CHAOS B_r continued down)
→ outer/inner core. The join has to state its seams. CRUST1.0 *replaces*
PREM's 1-D crust (15/24.4 km) above the local Moho, and PREM resumes below
it. That re-hangs PREM's lid, and §5 measures what it costs in mass.

## 5. Gates — the physics each dataset must reproduce

Every gate is an identity or a published measured number, and each gets a
NEGATIVE CONTROL that has to fail (repo rule: a threshold without a control
is a guess).

**PREM.** Integrated mass = 5.9722×10²⁴ kg, C/MR² = 0.3307 (observed); g at
the CMB ≈ 10.7 m/s²; central pressure ≈ 364 GPa; continuity across every
discontinuity where PREM says continuous. *Measured on the ObsPy nodes today,
with linear interpolation:* **M = 5.9755×10²⁴ kg, C/MR² = 0.3309.** The
transcribed polynomials must beat that, and match the 88 `prem.nd` nodes to
the file's printed precision. Negative control: drop the inner-core density
jump and C/MR² must move out of the band. Then **seismic rays**: tracing P
through Vp(r) must open the P shadow zone at ~103°–142°, checked against
`obspy.taup` PREM travel times at fixed distances (offline, baked oracle).

**CRUST1.0.** Layer tops monotonic and every thickness ≥ 0 in all 64 800
cells. Cell lookup reproduces the reference `getCN1point` at test points,
**index convention included** (row order from 89.5° N, −179.5° E; an
off-by-one cell is the classic silent bug). **Isostasy:** lithostatic
pressure at a 250 km compensation depth (CRUST1.0 column over PREM below the
Moho), compared across cells, should be far flatter than the topography's own
load. Report the measured scatter; do not tune toward a target. Negative
control: a constant-thickness crust under the same topography must scatter
much more.

**Slab2.** Depth-grid sign convention (Slab2 depths are negative km) and
0–360° longitude pinned. Then two emergent checks against data we already
hold. (1) **Arc front above the slab:** join the volcano catalogue's
subduction-zone volcanoes to Slab2 depth beneath each. The published
distribution is roughly 70–170 km, mean ~100 km (Syracuse & Abers 2006); we
report OUR measured distribution, with a ±1° shuffled-position control.
(2) **Live quakes:** label each USGS event as interface / intraslab / upper
plate by its depth against Slab2 ± `unc`, and only inside the grid. Outside
the grid it gets *no label*, never a guess.

**CFM.** NAD27 / UTM 11N → WGS84. The datum shift is ~100 m-class, below the
2000 m mesh spacing, but disclosed. Gates: round-trip a few fault traces
against the 5.3 `lonLat` traces in `SCECcode/CFM` (BSD-3, reachable), and
check that SoCal M≥2.5 seismicity sits measurably closer to CFM surfaces than
a shuffled control.

**CHAOS.** Our evaluator (through `igrf.js`) against ChaosMagPy at a grid of
points and epochs: nT agreement within the shc rounding. CHAOS-8 vs IGRF-14
at 2020.0 for n ≤ 13: a few nT, the known model difference, reported rather
than forced to zero. The **Lowes–Mauersberger knee** (core-dominated → crust
flat, ~n 14) becomes MEASURED from CHAOS core + lithosphere instead of the
sentence asserted in `core-model.js`. Secular variation / secular acceleration
at the CMB come from the spline derivatives. Geomagnetic jerks may be called
out **only where the model's own SA changes sign**, never from a literature
list. Past the last data epoch: the `_extrapolated` file, labelled as such.

## 8a. P1 as built — the geoelectric nowcast (2026-11-18)

**Chain:** `/api/geomag/observatories` (USGS variation, now X **and Y**) → `geoelectric-nowcast.js`
(column per station BY ID) → `geoelectric.js` (causal kernel per column, direct convolution of
the field's increments) → E_north, E_east, |E| per minute, warm-up/bridged flags, TPL-007 α/β
context. Nothing is invented: a station without Y is skipped, a gap > 5 min splits the series,
unknown E is null, no β is made up for a column NERC does not tabulate.

**Ground columns (38 + 2):** USGS ground-conductivity model v1.3 (21 physiographic regions, public
domain), GSC Open File 8115 (15 Canadian observatories, Open Government Licence – Canada, to
confirm), the NERC TPL-007 Quebec reference, BOU, plus Grayver 2017 global (land / with ocean).
Copied byte-for-byte from `greglucas/bezpy` @ b7944f9 (MIT) and CROSS-CHECKED layer by layer
against an independent copy in `butala/pyrsss` @ 5b9d347 (MIT): all 21 regions agree. Two traps
found and handled in the builder: BOU declares 11 layers but lists 12 (proved the same Earth both
ways), and pyrsss's FL1 has `5.0000+03` (missing `e`, repaired in the CROSS-CHECK copy only and
whitelisted exactly). Region polygons (which column applies where) are USGS's KMZ at
geomag.usgs.gov — NOT baked; until a refresh Action fetches it, a station gets (1) an explicit
column, (2) a model named for that observatory (BOU), or (3) the global average, flagged.

**The kernel, and the two versions that were measured wrong first:**
1. FFT of Z sampled on the 1-min grid → band-limited, 18–34 % of the kernel at negative lags,
   step response of the wrong sign (×9). 2. 16× oversampled FFT with B piecewise linear →
   causal, but the aliased tail converges as K^(−½) and left a constant offset. **Shipped:** the
   exact ramp response R(t) = (2/π)∫ Im Z/(μ₀ω²) sin ωt dω by Filon quadrature, then
   w[j] = (R((j+1)dt) − R(j·dt))/dt — exactly causal for B piecewise linear. Gated against the
   half-space closed form (2×10⁻⁴) and an independent Stehfest inverse-Laplace on layered
   columns (1.7×10⁻⁵).

**Measured error budget (all in the gate):** steady sinusoid vs the continuous |Z|: ≤ 0.2 % /
0.9° at 60 min, 0.7 % / 1.7° at 30 min, **2 % / 6.7° at 10 min** (reported, not gated) — the
piecewise-linear model of 1-min data, converging to the exact discrete transfer
Σ_p Z(f + p/dt)·sinc²(f·dt + p). Missing history after the 240-min warm-up: 2–10 % of rms |E|
(BR1, BOU worst); the nowcast fetches 720 min. 12 h memory cut: tail ≤ 5×10⁻⁶ V/km per nT.
**Cross-validation against NERC:** a |Z| band proxy ranks the 22 tabulated models like

## 10. Tomography — OUR OWN model (owner decision 2026-11-18)

Published tomography models are all licence-blocked for commercial use (none licensed; one GPL —
the evidence is kept in §10.9). So we build our own: **"PP-P1", a global P-wave travel-time
tomography of the mantle**, made from licence-clean travel-time picks with our own forward model
and inversion, shipped with its resolution map. Physics-first and auditable is also the
product's positioning (CLAUDE.md §7): every voxel traceable to rays, picks and a stated
regularisation.

### 10.1 What it will be — and what it will not

| It IS | It is NOT |
|---|---|
| Linearised P travel-time tomography: δt = ∫ δ(1/v) ds along PREM rays, solved for δVp/Vp on a mantle grid | Full-waveform / adjoint tomography (GLAD-M25 class) — a supercomputer problem |
| Long-wavelength (≳ 500–1000 km) mantle structure where rays cross | Uniform resolution: oceans and the southern hemisphere are poorly sampled, and the map says so |
| Shipped with checkerboard + spike resolution and a held-out skill score | A model with error bars per voxel that mean much (the null space is large; the resolution map is the honest uncertainty) |
| Our work product end to end (picks, ray tracer, inversion) | A re-hosting of anyone's model |

P (not S) first: P picks are 5–10× more numerous and far easier to make. Its price is stated up
front — the LLSVPs are SHEAR-velocity features and show only weakly in P; slabs show strongly.

### 10.2 Data — licence-clean first, permission-gated second

| Tier | Source | What | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **USGS ComCat** phase data (public domain) | NEIC hypocentres + their P arrivals/residuals for M ≥ ~5 global events | **UNVERIFIED** that teleseismic picks are exposed per event (earthquake.usgs.gov is egress-blocked here) — first job of T0 |
| A/B | **OUR OWN picks** from FDSN waveforms (EarthScope DMC, GEOFON, ORFEUS) | automatic P picks (STA/LTA → AIC refine) + cross-correlation differential times on GSN/global stations | picks are our product; the licence is per NETWORK (most FDSN network DOIs are CC BY 4.0; GSN II/IU open) — recorded per pick, a network without a commercial-OK licence is excluded |
| A | FDSN station web services | station coordinates, elevations | open metadata |
| C | **ISC Bulletin / ISC-EHB** | the standard reviewed catalogue (what most global P models are built from) — the single biggest coverage gain | terms to confirm in writing; add only after permission |

Scale target for v0: ~5 000 events (M ≥ 5.5, 1990–2026) × ~300 stations ≈ 10⁶ P picks, binned
into ~10⁵ summary rays (source/receiver cells), the standard way to equalise sampling.

### 10.3 Forward model — our own, on the P0 kernel

- **1-D ray tracer** through `prem.js` (and ak135 as an option): exact τ-p integration in each
  polynomial region, ray parameter p, Δ(p), T(p); handles the 220/400/670/CMB discontinuities by
  `side` (P0 already returns both sides of a jump).
- **Corrections** applied to every observed time: ellipticity (Kennett & Gudmundsson 1996
  coefficients — to transcribe and gate), station elevation, crust (CRUST1.0 once permitted;
  until then a STATION TERM absorbs it and the near-surface is not interpreted), event origin
  time/depth (joint relocation — event terms in the inversion).
- **Sensitivity**: ray-theoretical path lengths per cell (finite-frequency kernels are a later
  refinement, flagged).

### 10.4 Parametrisation and inversion

- Grid: ~2° equal-area cells × ~30 layers (surface to CMB) ≈ 3×10⁵ unknowns, plus station and
  event terms. Sparse G with ~200 cells per ray.
- Damped, smoothed least squares by LSQR (pure, in a Worker or the Action), Huber-weighted for
  outliers; regularisation chosen by L-curve AND held-out residuals, both recorded.
- One re-trace iteration through the updated model is optional and measured, not assumed.

### 10.5 Gates (each with a negative control, the repo rule)

1. **Ray tracer**: τ-p identities (dT/dΔ = p exactly), reciprocity, the P shadow zone opening at
   ~103° and core phases beyond, and travel times against ObsPy TauP on PREM — run IN the GitHub
   Action (pip works on runners; it does not in this sandbox), baked as a fixture.
2. **Synthetic recovery** with the REAL ray geometry: checkerboards at 3 scales and planted
   spikes; the recovered amplitude per cell IS the shipped resolution map. Control: random rays
   of the same count recover better than real ones (proves the map measures OUR coverage).
3. **Held-out skill**: 10 % of events withheld; the model must reduce their residual variance vs
   PREM-only by a stated amount. Control: the same inversion on shuffled residuals must not.
4. **Emergent geology** (never tuned toward): mean δVp inside Slab2 slabs at 200–660 km is fast
   vs a shuffled-slab control (P3's Slab2 bake is a dependency); cratons fast above 250 km; the
   lowermost mantle beneath Africa/the Pacific slow — reported as a measured (weak, P-wave)
   number, not asserted.
5. **Internal comparison only**: correlation by spherical-harmonic degree against published
   models computed on a networked machine, never shipped or redistributed.

### 10.6 Compute and pipeline

GitHub Actions (owner decision §0: Actions, zero GPU). T0 data audit + T2 pick download run as a
scheduled/ manual workflow writing to R2 (the Climalytix ingest pattern: R2 only, never commits,
never Supabase); the inversion runs as a manual workflow (ray tracing ~10⁶ rays ≈ minutes; LSQR
on ~10⁵ summary rays × 3×10⁵ unknowns in float32 fits a runner) and opens ONE PR with the baked
model + resolution + skill report (the trojans-refresh rule: never push to main).
