For researchers & operators

Built like an instrument, not a demo

Every simulation here names the model it runs, carries the data it runs on, and is pinned by a test you can read. Forecasts are locked before the event and scored against what actually arrived.

Published models, named on the page

NRLMSISE-00 for the thermosphere, IGRF-14 for the core field, Vallado's SGP4 for every orbit, Shue 1998 for the magnetopause, VSOP87 and Meeus for the planets, PFSS for the corona, TOV + SPH for the mergers. No model is a black box.

Forecasts locked, then scored

Each CME arrival forecast is locked at issue time and scored against the L1 shock when it comes. The skill ledger is public, and a forecast issued after the shock is never counted as a hit.

Provenance on every number

Archival imagery is labelled archival, live feeds carry their freshness, and a dead feed says it is dead rather than drawing a quiet planet. Model output is marked model wherever it sits next to a measurement.

Kernels you can test

The physics lives in pure modules with no DOM and no network, each pinned by its own gate — identities, closed forms and negative controls that must fail. The Moon's interior reproduces the measured moment of inertia; Pinatubo through the volcanic climate chain gives the observed forcing.
