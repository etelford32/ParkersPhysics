Seismic rays through PREM

Rays leave a source at equal steps of takeoff angle and bend by Snell's law in PREM's velocities. The liquid outer core slows P waves and stops S waves outright — that is what carves the shadow zones. Hover the travel-time chart (or the surface of the cross-section) to read every arrival at that distance.

Ray theory in PREM (isotropic, no ocean — as TauP), 5 km shells with exact in-shell integrals; gated against closed forms, RK4 eikonal shooting and quadrature on PREM's polynomials (tests/earth-interior-raytrace.mjs). Rays leave the source downward only, so a deep source's nearest distances (reached by up-going rays) are not drawn. This fan draws the direct and core phases; the reflected, converted, depth, multiple and diffracted phases (PcP, ScS, ScP, pP, sP, PP, SS, SKS, Pdiff…) are traced in the live view above with ellipticity corrected (tests/earth-interior-phases.mjs). No amplitudes. This is step T1 of our own tomography model: the forward operator a tomographic inversion is built on.

What this is — and is not

  • Structure is a reference model. PREM (Dziewonski & Anderson 1981) is spherically averaged: no slabs, plumes or continents. Lateral structure (CRUST1.0, Slab2, our own tomography) is planned — see the roadmap.
  • Conductivity is a global average. Grayver et al. (2017), inverted from satellite and observatory data. Real ground near the surface varies by orders of magnitude.
  • The electric field is modelled, not measured. A plane-wave source over a 1-D layered ground (the NERC TPL-007 method). No coasts or 3-D structure; E is computed at the observatory where B is measured, not along a power line.
  • Ground columns are chosen by id. USGS v1.3 regions, NRCan observatory models, the NERC Quebec reference, or the flagged global default. USGS's region map is not yet baked, so nothing infers a column from a location.
  • Benchmarks are context. TPL-007's 8·α·β V/km is a 1-in-100-year planning field, shown only where NERC tabulates β for the column.
