- **D_p is the PROPER libration amplitude**: the amplitude of the fundamental libration line of φ = λ − λ_Jupiter found by frequency analysis (NAFF), summed in quadrature over its secular sidebands — the definition AstDyS publishes, and one that does NOT grow with run length (§12). "Half-range" is the old statistic, half the peak-to-peak φ over the run (median 0.22° wider than D_p, and growing with run length). The period is 360°/f from the same analysis. The "analytic" amplitude is the averaged planar-circular estimate from one epoch.

### Who is right where g disagrees? The g-vs-D trend test (20000-yr run)

The free g of a Trojan RISES with its libration amplitude, and on the objects where both agree it climbs smoothly with D_p. A disputed value (|Δg| > 20″/yr) that continues that trend is the physical one.

Above D_p ≈ 15° our disputed g continues the agreed trend while AstDyS's falls far below it (its worst cases sit near Saturn's g6 ≈ 28″/yr with an almost identical e ≈ 0.030 across unrelated objects — the signature of a forced line taken as free). Verdicts here are inference from a trend, not proof — a ≥ 250-kyr run with all four outer planets on a disputed sample would settle them (plan §4).
