The L4 (leading, "Greek") swarm is the larger one, as every survey has found. The split above is the first hint at how much of that is discovery history: the ratio among SHORT-ARC orbits (recent, barely followed-up discoveries) is far larger than among well-determined ones, i.e. recent surveying has favoured L4.
