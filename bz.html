    What this is

    Most space-weather displays show one number: Bz from one spacecraft, a million and a half kilometres upstream. This page follows that field down. Three spacecraft at L1 are fused into one value with an error bar. Physics carries it to the bow shock and across it. Two GOES satellites measure what the magnetosphere did with it. Fourteen USGS observatories measure what reached the ground, once a second.

    Nothing here is a simulation of the magnetosphere. Where there is an instrument, you see the instrument. Where there is none, you see an estimate with its uncertainty and a note saying what it leaves out. One rung, low Earth orbit, is not built: no public instrument reports there in real time.
